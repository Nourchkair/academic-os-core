## Delivery-mode strategy

Set the course-level and session-level modes only from authoritative evidence:

- **In Person:** prioritize preparation before class, attendance, professor emphasis/examples, handwritten or personal notes, and prompt post-class review.
- **Online — Synchronous:** prioritize live attendance, platform/link readiness, lecture notes, professor emphasis, and post-session review.
- **Online — Asynchronous:** prioritize module/completion tracking, internal study targets, scheduled self-study blocks, avoiding accumulation, and active recall after each module. Do not fabricate a class meeting time.
- **Hybrid:** apply the appropriate strategy separately to each session/component; do not collapse the course into one boolean.
- **Unknown:** do not guess. Record the evidence needed to resolve the mode and flag it in course status/briefs.
